# Onboarding checklist

Owner, 2026-10-28: "ok lets keep moving" (next step after go-live: the onboarding checklist). Built from the company's "New Hire Checklist" (Google Drive).

## How it works
- **Starts automatically** for every account created in Accounts. Managers can start one for an existing person: Accounts → "Start onboarding checklist".
- **Office (full access):**
  - Signed offer letter received
  - Form I-9 completed and documents checked (in person, within 3 business days)
  - W-4 and DE-4 completed (payroll)
  - Direct deposit set up (payroll)
  - Emergency contact on file
  - EEOC self-identification offered (optional: voluntary, kept separate)
  - Added to payroll and QuickBooks Time
- **Supervisor (their manager, or full access):** first day scheduled, PPE and uniform ready, locker and time clock set up, safety walk-through done.
- **New hire (themselves, or their manager):** bring ID documents for the I-9; finish payroll setup in QuickBooks Workforce (with a link).
- **Ticked by the Hub:** account and own PIN; Face ID on the phone (optional); company documents signed; first weekly report.
- **Who sees what:**
  - The new hire gets a **"Your first week"** card on Home with their own items and the automatic ones, plus a count of what the office and supervisor still have.
  - Managers get a Home to-do per new hire who has items waiting on them, and **More → Onboarding** shows everyone in progress.
- **Finishing:** the checklist completes by itself when every required item is done. Full access can close or reopen it.
- **Privacy:** only ticks are stored (who, what, when). I-9 documents, tax forms, bank details and EEOC answers are never entered in the Hub. The screens say so.
- Everything is audited and included in the backup.
- Add a person asks "Who is this?": **Already works here** (preselected, no new-hire checklist) or **New hire** (starts the onboarding checklist). A checklist can still be started later from Accounts.

## Rolling out to current staff (owner 2026-10-28: "what do we do to onboard existings?")
- Not in the Hub yet: Add a person ("Already works here"), then the welcome email or the invite QR code in person (a shop meeting is fastest).
- Added but the temporary PIN was never used: Send welcome email / Show invite QR code.
- Already using their own PIN: nothing to invite. Home shows "Set up this phone", and published documents appear as "Please read and sign".
